### **Problem Statement & Understanding (20 pts)**

#### **The Ocean Crisis We Face:**
Our oceans are facing unprecedented threats that require immediate action:

- **8 million tons** of plastic waste enter our oceans annually
- **700+ marine species** are affected by plastic pollution
- Ocean temperatures have risen **1.2°C above normal** in the Indian Ocean
- **Sri Lankan coastal waters** are particularly vulnerable due to industrial waste from rivers like Kelani
- **Marine biodiversity** in places like Hikkaduwa and Mirissa is under threat

#### **Local Context - Sri Lanka:**
- Kelani River pollution directly affects Colombo's coastline
- Critical turtle nesting sites in Kosgoda and Rekawa need protection
- Coral reefs in Hikkaduwa require conservation efforts
- Beach pollution affects tourism and marine ecosystems

#### **Our Solution:**
Save The Sea 2.0 is a comprehensive mobile application that tackles ocean conservation through:
1. **Awareness Building** - Educational content about local marine issues
2. **Behavior Change** - Gamified challenges promoting eco-friendly habits
3. **Community Action** - Connecting like-minded individuals for collective impact
4. **Data-Driven Insights** - Real-time ocean health alerts and statistics
